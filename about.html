About Calloway Holdings

Dedicated to delivering institutional-grade facility operations, rigorous quality standards, and dependable management for commercial real estate portfolios.

Our Foundation

Built on Accountability and Operational Excellence

Calloway Holdings was established to solve a persistent issue in commercial property management: inconsistent service delivery and poor operational accountability. We bridge the gap between facility directors and site crews by maintaining strict oversight on every job site.

From daily commercial janitorial contracts to large-scale event venue sanitation, our standardized operational procedures ensure your facilities stay pristine, safe, and welcoming for occupants and visitors every single day.

Guiding Principles

The Calloway Standards

1. Consistent Execution

We do not cut corners. Every site protocol, checklist, and quality audit is executed strictly according to schedule, regardless of facility size.

2. Vetted & Insured Teams

Our operational staff and contracted specialist crews undergo background verification and safety compliance training before stepping onto your property.

3. Direct Communication

No endless phone trees. Facility managers receive direct access to dedicated account superintendents for rapid issue resolution.

4. Scalable Capacity

Our growing nationwide brokerage network enables rapid expansion into specialized verticals—from fleet care to post-construction cleanup.
