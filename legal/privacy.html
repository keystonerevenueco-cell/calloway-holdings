1. Scope & Platform Governance

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) governs the data collection, processing, storage, and protection practices of Calloway Holdings (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) across all digital platforms, web properties, mobile portals, and commercial service contracts. This document applies to all enterprise clients, facility managers, property owners, platform users, and contractors accessing our services.

By engaging Calloway Holdings, interacting with our digital interfaces, or submitting service inquiries, you acknowledge and agree to the terms set forth in this Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, you must cease use of our digital platforms and commercial services immediately.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

Calloway Holdings collects personal and proprietary corporate information strictly necessary to execute commercial facility operations, manage client accounts, process billing, and fulfill contractual obligations.

  • Corporate Contact Data: Full names, job titles, business emails, direct phone numbers, and physical facility service addresses.
  • Operational & Facility Specifications: Building square footage, floor plans, entry access codes, security protocols, site photo documentation, and specialized sanitation requirements.
  • Financial & Billing Information: Corporate billing addresses, tax identification numbers, primary accounts payable contacts, and payment authorization metadata.
  • Automated Telemetry & System Logs: IP addresses, browser specifications, access timestamps, referral URLs, device identifiers, and platform navigation metrics collected via web interaction.

3. Purpose & Execution of Data Usage

Calloway Holdings processes collected data exclusively for legal, administrative, and operational purposes directly related to enterprise commercial performance:

  • Generating customized commercial estimates, proposals, and service level agreements (SLAs).
  • Scheduling, dispatching, and auditing site crews, field technicians, and facility management teams.
  • Executing financial transactions, automated billing, invoice generation, and account reconciliation.
  • Issuing critical operational updates, emergency facility notifications, and quality assurance reports.
  • Fulfilling statutory compliance, insurance audits, OSHA reporting, and legal obligations.
Enterprise Data Protection Guarantee: Calloway Holdings does NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize client data, corporate contact rosters, or facility details to third-party marketing brokers or advertising networks under any circumstances.

4. Payment Gateways & Financial Security Disclaimers

All electronic transactions and card processing are executed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (such as Stripe). Calloway Holdings does not directly store, process, or transmit raw, unencrypted credit card numbers, CVVs, or sensitive banking credentials on local servers.

Third-Party Financial Liability Disclaimer: Calloway Holdings disclaims all direct liability for unauthorized transactions, data breaches, or processing failures occurring within third-party payment processor networks. Clients are subject to the privacy policies and security frameworks enforced by the respective payment processing vendor.

5. Subcontractors, Partners & Authorized Third Parties

To deliver multi-vertical commercial services, Calloway Holdings may share relevant operational data with vetted third-party operational partners, specialized subcontractors, field supervisors, and cloud infrastructure vendors.

Subcontractor Limitation of Data Liability: While Calloway Holdings executes strict non-disclosure and service agreements with all field partners, Calloway Holdings is not liable for independent torts, unauthorized disclosures, or data mishandling committed by third-party subcontractors outside the express written instructions of the Company.

6. Cookies & Automated Tracking Technologies

Our digital platforms utilize standard cookies, web beacons, and analytical tags to optimize user session management, evaluate portal navigation, and enhance operational security.

Users may adjust browser settings to block or disable cookies; however, doing so may impact functional access to administrative client portals, electronic proposal signatures, and digital request forms.

7. Enterprise Data Security & Absolute Warranty Disclaimer

Calloway Holdings maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect corporate data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include SSL/TLS encryption, restricted access protocols, and enterprise firewalls.

Limitation of Cybersecurity Warranty: No electronic transmission or storage repository is 100% secure. Calloway Holdings provides no absolute or express warranty against malicious cyberattacks, system breaches, zero-day vulnerabilities, or unauthorized third-party intrusion. Clients transmit information at their own risk.

8. Client Data Rights & Regional Regulations

Depending on geographical jurisdiction, commercial clients and individuals retain specific rights regarding their stored personal data, including the right to request access, correction, or formal erasure of non-operational records.

Data deletion requests will be honored except where retention is legally mandated for tax compliance, active contract enforcement, accounting audits, or ongoing dispute resolution.
